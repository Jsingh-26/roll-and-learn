## Art and voice through Composio Instant (31 Oct 2026)

The claude.ai Composio connector's Instant apps can make assets without a new account: ElevenLabs for
counting voice lines and sound effects ($0.08 per 1,000 characters), Black Forest Labs / Fal.ai for
illustrations ($0.04 per small image). Paid from the owner's free credit: get the owner's OK per batch,
commit the generated files into `assets/`, never call these services from the app itself. Full list and
rules: the global `composio-instant` skill.
